From Bottles to High-Quality rPET Flakes

1. Collection

Post-consumer PET bottles are collected.

2. Sorting

Bottles are sorted by color and type.

3. Washing

Hot washing removes labels, glue and dirt.

4. Drying

Moisture is removed for clean flakes.

5. Flaking

Bottles are shredded into uniform flakes.

6. Quality Control

Strict testing ensures consistent quality.

7. High-Quality rPET Flakes

Ready for your production.
