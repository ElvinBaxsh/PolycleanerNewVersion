Process & Quality

At Poly Cleaner MMC, our integrated process and quality systems ensure high quality rPET flakes with consistent specifications, full traceability and reliable export documentation.

Request Product Information
1

Collection

Post-consumer PET bottles are collected from trusted sources.

2

Sorting

Bottles are sorted by color and material; labels and contaminants removed.

3

Washing

Hot wash with eco-friendly detergents to remove dirt, glue and residues.

4

Drying

Centrifugal and thermal drying to reach optimal moisture.

5

Quality Control

In-process and final inspection to ensure consistent quality.

6

Packing & Loading

Flakes are packed in bulk bags and loaded for worldwide shipment.

Process Capabilities

Our facility is designed for efficient, sustainable and scalable rPET flake production.

  • Advanced sorting and color separation lines
  • Multi-stage hot washing system
  • High-efficiency friction & floating sink separation
  • Automated drying and air classification
  • Metal detection and final inspection
  • Bulk packing in 1100–1250 kg big bags
  • Daily production with strict process control

Quality Control & Documentation

Every batch is tested and documented to meet international buyer requirements.

  • Moisture content testing
  • PVC, labels & glue content analysis
  • Color and visual inspection
  • Contamination and foreign material check
  • Batch traceability from input to shipment
  • COA (Certificate of Analysis) for every shipment
  • Full export documentation support

Our Quality Assurance Pillars

Consistent Quality

Standardized processes and advanced equipment ensure stable, high-quality rPET flakes batch after batch.

Traceable Operations

End-to-end traceability from collection to shipment for complete visibility and accountability.
